OpenCV Zoo face models
  Source revision: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/48b10fa505aeb51fbd127fc60a5c660040e823b7
  The app downloads the YuNet and SFace models from this revision and verifies
  their hashes before use. Check the upstream model terms before redistribution.

Fonts
  Inter, Cinzel, and Cormorant Garamond are included under the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1. Their license texts are in fonts/OFL-*.txt.

Python dependencies
  Exact direct versions are listed in requirements.txt. Their licenses and
  transitive dependencies should be reviewed when distributing a binary build.
